Hunt for the key word.

A free reading game for pupils aged 10 to 12. Read the question, search the story, choose the answer.

Play the game

Three moves for every question.

Press a step to see it in the game.

Read the question. A key word is a name or a thing, such as "hat" or "Zara".

Try the hunt.

Type a word. Every match in the story turns yellow, just like in the game.

Play it now.

Type a nickname, press Start, and begin with Easy. The story is "A Day at Sea".

Open the game full screenPlay all three stories

Made for the classroom.

Teachers bring their own stories. Make one in any AI chat, drop the file on the start page, and the game checks it.

The Teacher panel then shows every pair's results and the mistakes the class makes most.
