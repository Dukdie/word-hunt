Hunt for the key word.

Word Hunt is a free reading game for pupils aged 10 to 12. Read the question, search the story, choose the answer.

Play the game Read the user guide

Three levels. Works on phones and laptops. Nothing to install.

Try it now

Type a nickname, press Start, then choose Easy. The story is "A Day at Sea".

Open the game full screen Play all three stories

How it works

Three steps for every question.

  1. 1

    Pick a key word

    Read the question. Choose one name or thing, such as "hat" or "Zara".

  2. 2

    Search for it

    Matching words turn yellow in the story. Read the sentences around them.

  3. 3

    Choose the answer

    Pupils get 3 tries. A wrong answer gets a short reason, so the next try is smarter.

For teachers

Use your own stories. Make one with the game's prompt in any AI chat, then paste the reply or drop the story file on the start page. The game checks it for you.

The Teacher panel shows every pair's results and the most common mistakes in the class.
